It all started with a simple question:

Why do we sometimes wake up tired, even after a full night's sleep?

Why do we wake up with a headache? And what happens during those hours we spend asleep, without ever knowing? We spend nearly a third of our lives sleeping, yet so much of it remains beyond our awareness and control.

That curiosity became SDeep. We are a team of six, bringing together programming, research, electronics, and mechanical design to create a smart pillow that doesn't just stay beside you while you sleep — it listens, learns, monitors, and responds.

Sleep should feel simple, personal, and within reach.
